# Reporting a security issue

If you believe you have found a vulnerability in KIVO, the app at app.onekivo.com or anything under onekivo.com, please tell us privately before publishing it.

- Email: info@onekivo.com, subject "Security".
- Include what you found, where, and the steps to reproduce it. A proof of concept against your own account or a free organization is welcome; please do not access other customers' data.
- We acknowledge within two working days and keep you informed until the issue is fixed.

Please do not run denial-of-service tests, spam or social engineering against our systems or our people.

What we protect and how is described on https://onekivo.com/en/security/.
