KIVO runs projects, timesheets and billing, people and leave, expenses, devices and the numbers, as one system with one source of truth. Made for consultancies, agencies and IT service providers that have outgrown spreadsheets and five disconnected tools.

- **Run projects.** Contracts, staffing, rates and hours. From an hour to an invoice number, with client sign-off that needs no account.
- **Look after people.** One record per person, contracts, documents, leave and sick days, with balances computed from the contract and the schedule.
- **Run the business.** Receipts read once, devices with a holder and a place, news, seats and permissions.
- **Know the numbers.** Twelve months of revenue and cost, computed from the same rows the work was booked in.

Hosted on EU infrastructure, one encryption key per customer, Postgres row-level security per tenant. The details are on the [security page](https://onekivo.com/en/security/).
